# SLINGSHOT — difficulty baseline

Generated by `tools/difficulty.mjs` 1.0.0. Re-run with `node tools/difficulty.mjs --write`.

This is analysis, not a regression test. None of it reaches `golden.json`, and none of it is imported by the game.

## Superseded numbers — do not quote these

Three figures circulated before the tool was corrected. They are wrong, and they
are recorded here so that nobody finds them in an old commit and treats them as
current.

| superseded | corrected | why it was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| `orbit` angle tolerance ~ +/-2.4 deg | **+/-12.62 deg** | 30 power samples put the representative winning launch on a narrow ledge. The expert-slot argument in  turns on this number. |
| `two-planets` has "32 strategies" | **3 meaningful regions of 37 components** | connected grid fragments were counted as routes; 34 of them are shards of a chaotic boundary, too small to aim at |
| search medians from before 2026-09-23 | see the table below | seeds derived from a level's index in the array being measured, so the same level scored differently depending on what it was measured alongside. Seeds now derive from the level id. |

The first two were found by re-measuring at higher density; the third by
measuring one level twice in different company and getting 86 and 41.

## How it was measured

| setting | value |
|---|---|
| launch grid | 360 angles over 360 deg x 120 powers from 12 to each level's cap |
| search trials | 40 per level, seeded from 20260923 |
| search budget | 2000 shots before a trial counts as failed |
| tolerance | walked outward from the most interior winning sample, 240 steps, each direction separately |

The search uses the same acceptance rule as the shipped bot — only ever move to a smaller miss — but starts from a seeded guess rather than one fixed one, so the spread shows how much of a shot count belongs to the level rather than to where the search began.
