SLINGSHOT

current

One push, and the planets do the rest.

Drag back and let go — everything after that is Newtonian gravity with nothing added. Seven levels; by the last one your engine is provably too weak to leave, and the only way out is a gravity assist off a moving moon.

THROUGH

You can walk through walls. Sometimes.

An ordinary platformer in which you are a quantum particle. Every wall wears its real tunnelling odds, T = exp(−2κd). A wall twice as thick is not twice as hard — it squares the odds.

SHIM

A 500 MHz magnet you have to get to spec.

A real instrument drill on a Bloch simulation: the lineshape tells you which shim is wrong, and you work Z1, Z2 and Z3 until the peak is worth acquiring on.

AGREEMENT

A game about things that drift apart.

Spins fan out because the field is uneven; a 180° pulse turns that drift around and brings them back together. The spin echo, played rather than derived.

PIXEL PLUMBER

where it started

Run, jump, stomp, reach the flag.

The original Mario-style platformer, built for a phone and still exactly as it was: touch controls, coins, goombas, a flagpole. Later it grew 🌙 low-gravity and 🧊 no-friction zones, with the live numbers behind the 🔬 button.

BLOCH SANDBOX

tool, not a game

Free play on the engine underneath.

No levels and no score — pulses, delays and a Bloch sphere, verified against closed form (a Hahn echo peaks at 35.0 ms against a predicted 34.6).
